Human Identification Guide

Over 12,000,000 instances of strange strings of malicious code have tampered with personal data spanning from almost a billion of users each year. From public distribution of personal files and photos, sending unauthorized texts to random or risky numbers, to outright impersonation of a user via Video Call. Furthermore, there is a more peculiar outcome to those who have reached out to special services. 25% of users who have called special services were wiped out of all databases within hours after their statement. It is also known that users who interact with a tampered account soon end up getting affected by the string afterwards.
